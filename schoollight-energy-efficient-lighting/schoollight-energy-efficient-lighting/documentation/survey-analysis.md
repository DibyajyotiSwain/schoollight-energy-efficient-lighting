# Survey charts and limitations

The original presentation contains survey charts on slides 5–7. The chart images are preserved in `assets/images/`.

## Topics covered

The charts ask about:

- Lighting type considered most conducive to learning or work.
- School areas where current lighting is most lacking or most effective.
- Perceived impact of current lighting on mood and productivity.
- Preferences regarding lighting colour temperature.
- Interest in smart lighting controls that adjust based on occupancy and natural-light availability.

## Response counts visible in the source charts

- Slides 5 and 6: five responses are shown for each chart.
- Slide 7: five responses are shown for the colour-temperature question and three responses for the smart-controls question.

## Interpretation limits

These are small exploratory samples. The charts can help illustrate the questions asked and prompt further investigation, but they cannot support broad claims about the whole school community. The source presentation does not include a raw response export, participant demographics, sampling method, survey dates, or a detailed analysis method.

For that reason, this repository preserves the charts as source material and avoids presenting percentages as statistically representative findings. A stronger follow-up study should define the target population, recruit a larger and more diverse sample, retain anonymized raw responses, and report the survey method and limitations.
