# Introduction

## Background

School lighting supports learning, movement, and day-to-day campus activities. The 2023 presentation considers how conventional lighting in educational settings could be improved through energy-efficient choices and a more user-centred design process.

## Project aim

The aim is to outline a conceptual framework for an optimized school lighting system, with attention to reduced energy consumption, a comfortable learning environment, and the needs of students, teachers, and staff.

## Scope

The source presentation explicitly frames the work as a design exercise rather than a physical implementation report. It presents stakeholder feedback, design goals, a smart-lighting concept, a planning blueprint, area-specific lighting proposals, and a proposed pilot and evaluation plan.

## Intended outcomes

- Match lighting choices to the use of each school area.
- Consider comfort, safety, adaptability, and energy efficiency together.
- Explore automated controls and renewable-energy options.
- Establish a basis for a future pilot that could be evaluated using energy data and user feedback.

## Important distinction

The presentation includes an implementation plan, but does not document completed installation, measured energy savings, or final user-test results. Those activities are therefore described in this repository as proposed next steps.
