# Proposed implementation and evaluation plan

The original presentation outlines a pilot followed by a gradual expansion. It does not establish that these activities were completed.

## Proposed pilot

- **Initial area:** IBCP Block.
- **Classrooms:** Energy-efficient LED bulbs.
- **Corridors:** Motion-sensor-activated LED bulbs.
- **Laboratories and auditorium:** Solar-powered LED lighting.
- **Evaluation:** Monitor energy consumption, lighting quality, and user feedback.
- **Iteration:** Adjust the design based on findings.

## Proposed expansion

If a pilot demonstrated acceptable performance, the presentation proposes a phased rollout to other school areas, including the auditorium, IGCSE Block, and CBSE Block. Solutions would be tailored to each area's requirements, with ongoing monitoring and feedback.

## Suggested evaluation measures

These measures are recommendations for a future pilot; they are not results reported by the original project.

| Area | Suggested measure | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | kWh consumed by zone, before and during pilot | Establishes whether energy use changes |
| Lighting | Illuminance (lux) at relevant work surfaces | Checks whether lighting is adequate and consistent |
| Controls | Sensor activation and timeout logs | Helps identify false triggers or unnecessary operation |
| User experience | Structured feedback from students and staff | Checks comfort, usability, and perceived safety |
| Reliability | Faults, downtime, and maintenance events | Assesses operational practicality |
| Solar system | Generation, battery state, and backup duration | Checks whether the proposed backup arrangement is appropriately sized |

## Before any real-world installation

A qualified facilities/electrical team should assess the building, wiring, fixture compatibility, emergency-lighting requirements, fire and electrical safety, local regulations, procurement, and maintenance. Solar and battery systems need load calculations and appropriate protection. The concept should not be treated as an installation specification.
