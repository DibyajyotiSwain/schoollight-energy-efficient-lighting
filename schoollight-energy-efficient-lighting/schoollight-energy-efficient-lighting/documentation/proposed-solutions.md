# Proposed lighting solutions

The project proposes matching a lighting approach to the function of each school space.

## Classrooms: energy-efficient LED bulbs

**Features described in the presentation**
- Low energy consumption
- Long lifespan
- Adjustable brightness

**Intended benefits**
- Consistent and comfortable lighting during lessons
- Support for a conducive learning environment
- Potentially lower maintenance needs over time

## Corridors: motion-sensor-activated LED bulbs

**Features described in the presentation**
- Motion detection
- Energy-saving mode
- Adjustable sensitivity

**Intended benefits**
- Illumination when movement is detected
- Reduced unnecessary lighting during idle periods
- Improved visibility and safety in circulation areas

## Laboratories and auditorium: solar-powered LED bulbs

**Features described in the presentation**
- Solar-panel integration
- Battery backup
- Remote monitoring

**Intended benefits**
- Use of renewable energy
- Backup lighting during grid interruptions, subject to system sizing
- A visible example of renewable-energy adoption

## Personalized smart-lighting concept

The ideation slide proposes controls that let users adjust brightness, colour temperature, and lighting patterns for their area or activity. Automated sensors are proposed to support efficient operation. The source does not specify a complete control architecture, sensor bill of materials, electrical design, or validated performance figures.

## Design considerations before deployment

A real installation would require a lighting survey, lux-level targets, fixture selection, electrical and fire-safety review, sensor placement, solar and battery sizing, accessibility considerations, maintenance planning, and a cost-benefit assessment. These are future engineering tasks, not completed project results.
